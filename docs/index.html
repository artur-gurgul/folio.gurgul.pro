folio

One subject, one A4 page, as dense as it gets.

Each folio is a page for remembering a subject: the important facts, and the pictures that carry them, packed onto a single printable A4 sheet. No filler, no narrative, nothing to skip. Read it here, or print it — every folio also comes as a one-page PDF.

  • Dense — every line is a fact worth keeping.
  • Visual — a diagram wherever a picture says it better than words.
  • Linked — folios are tagged and point to related folios, so a subject connects to its neighbours.

A folio is not interview preparation, not a tutorial and not a blog post. It is the page you keep next to you.
